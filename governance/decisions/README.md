# Decision register

Material product, architecture, budget, security, data, or production decisions are
recorded here with both founders' decisions. Routine implementation choices are
owned by the CPTO and delivery teams and do not wait for founder review.

Every record states the context, alternatives, decision owner, rationale, impact,
date, and evidence. A material disagreement blocks only the affected work and is
escalated to Ankit and Manu; unrelated work continues.
