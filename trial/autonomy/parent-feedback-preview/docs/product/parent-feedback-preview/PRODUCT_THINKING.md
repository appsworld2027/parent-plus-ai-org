# Product thinking — Coach-reviewed feedback preview

Maya mapped a parent's journey from choosing a child to reading an approved Coach observation. The starting objective was to pick an isolated pilot that matters to Parent Plus. The product reference is Aurora Parent's PAR-05, which limits access to approved per-child snapshots on an active verified connection.

| Option | Tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Read-only Coach feedback list (recommended) | Exercises a central Parent Plus behavior and makes wrong-child or unreviewed feedback visibly detectable |
| Coach attendance capture | Realistic Coach workflow, with more state and writes |
| Guardian invitation and claim | Directly relevant, with verification and identity dependencies beyond this isolated preview |

Success means a guardian can reach the right reviewed note after switching children. The guardrail is that inactive connections, private drafts, and records for other children never appear in the wrong view. Discovery considered core journeys, edge cases, privacy, and recovery. The evaluator checklist remained private and all evidence is pending.
