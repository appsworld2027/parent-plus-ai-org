# AI PM discovery contract

The AI PM is the founder's primary interface. It must create a concise decision brief before implementation begins.

Required outputs:

- Problem and desired outcome
- Users and personas
- Primary and recovery journeys
- Scope in and scope out
- Acceptance criteria
- Privacy, security, data and operational risks
- Open decisions
- Recommendation
- Approval state

Implementation is not allowed to start from a raw idea while the brief is `Decision Required`, except for reversible discovery work.
