# AI Org — Session Checkpoint

The AI fills this in; the founder never does. Use at every session, tool or model switch.
(Stage-to-stage packets between background agents keep the `scripts/handoff.mjs` format.)
Write "Unknown" where there is no evidence. No secrets or personal data.

## 1. Current objective
What are we trying to achieve?
## 2. Approved decisions
What has been decided? Include the reason and approval reference.
## 3. Completed work
What has been completed, with evidence?
## 4. Current implementation state
Relevant files, changes, dependencies and outstanding work.
## 5. Quality evidence
Tests completed, results, unresolved failures and known regressions.
## 6. Constraints and known traps
What must not change? What mistakes must not be repeated?
## 7. Open decisions
What remains unresolved, and who must decide?
## 8. Resource consumption
Model, available token metrics, subscription utilization, retries and elapsed time.
## 9. Next action
The exact next task for the receiving session.
## 10. Memory updates
New product knowledge or decisions to persist beyond this feature.
